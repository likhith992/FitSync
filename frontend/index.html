FitSync Weight Management Guide

Building Muscle

  • Follow regular resistance training.
  • Consume sufficient protein and calories.
  • Gradually increase training intensity.
  • Prioritize adequate recovery and sleep.

Fat Loss

  • Maintain an appropriate calorie deficit.
  • Keep protein intake adequate.
  • Include resistance training.
  • Maintain regular physical activity.

Healthy Weight Gain

  • Consume an appropriate calorie surplus.
  • Include protein-rich foods.
  • Focus on strength training.
  • Monitor your progress regularly.
